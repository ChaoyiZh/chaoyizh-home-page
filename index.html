Chaoyi Zhou

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Clemson University advised by Prof. Siyu Huang. Previously, I obtained my M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Southern California, where I was advised by Prof. Yajie Zhao. I received my B.E. in Computer Science and Technology from Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications in 2020.

My research focuses on 3D and 4D reconstruction, scene representations, and geometric foundation models. I develop methods that recover scene geometry, motion, and semantics from images and videos, with applications in general scene understanding and endoscopic video analysis.
